Why High Altitude Changes Everything

High Altitude (HAA) and Super High Altitude Areas (SHAA) present critical environmental stressors that compromise conventional electrical/electronic hardware.

❄️

Subzero Temperatures

Causes lubricant freezing, component embrittlement, and increased semiconductor thresholds.

Engineering Impact: Increased starting torque, mechanical binding, structural fatigue.
📉

Low Atmospheric Pressure

Reduces air density, severely lowering dielectric breakdown voltage and insulating capacity.

Engineering Impact: Corona discharge risks, internal arcing, insulation degradation.
🌡️

Thermal Cycling

Extreme diurnal variations lead to expansion and contraction stress across mechanical joints.

Engineering Impact: Micro-fractures in PCB solder joints and seal failure.
☀️

Intense Solar/UV Exposure

High UV radiation accelerates degradation of exposed polymers, wiring insulation, and enclosures.

Engineering Impact: Material embrittlement and surface insulation loss.
🌬️

Reduced Convective Cooling

Rarified air removes significantly less heat per unit volume than sea-level atmosphere.

Engineering Impact: Thermal runaway in power electronics despite subzero ambient air.
⚡

Electrical Drift

Temperature-dependent resistance shifts affect current draw, efficiency, and timing circuits.

Engineering Impact: Dynamic operational instability and uncalibrated sensor drift.

Project Objective

Official Disclaimer

This website represents a student/hackathon prototype developed for educational and demonstration purposes inspired by iDEX Problem Statement 26049. Environmental values, performance metrics, and equipment behavior displayed in demonstration screens are simulated unless explicitly identified as experimentally measured on physical hardware. This project is not certified by or formally deployed by DRDO or military authorities.
